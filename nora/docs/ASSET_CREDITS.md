# Resource imagery and links · NORA V2

In this source export, the files listed below live under
`src/mental_health_pipeline/web_nora/assets/`, rather than `dist/assets/`.
The unselected sample collections are hidden in the connected interface.

Photos are bundled locally so opening the design does not send requests to image providers. Actual resource/video pages open only when their external link is chosen. Photographs illustrate topics; they do not imply endorsement by the people shown.
